# Brainstorm Brief For Next Algorithm Ideas

Date: 2026-07-01

Use this file as a prompt for an external ChatGPT-style brainstorming pass. The goal is to propose next algorithm improvements for the current compact beam + QUBO CPMP solver.

## Prompt To External ChatGPT

You are helping improve a CPMP / container pre-marshalling solver. The current promoted research line is a compact byte-state beam search with a small QUBO quality-diversity selector and BS-FRG-inspired macro-chain witnesses. QUBO only selects among legal child states or replayable macro-chain candidates generated by classical search. It does not generate crane moves.

Please propose ablation-friendly next-step ideas that can improve all three surfaces:

1. open120 DLTS 5x5/7x5 results;
2. paper BF results;
3. paper CV results.

Important constraints:

- Do not propose deep learning or trained policies.
- Do not propose running multiple solvers as a fallback portfolio.
- Do not propose case-name-specific gates.
- Do not turn BS-FRG into a fallback solver.
- Prefer interpretable mechanisms that can be switched on/off and tested.
- Any proposed mechanism should preserve legal move generation and validator replay.
- Any candidate must be paired against `qubo_scheduler_state_v5_qubo_diversity_union_macro_chain_witness_polish`.

Please return:

1. the top 5 ideas, ranked by expected value and risk;
2. a short mechanism sketch for each idea;
3. what telemetry would prove or disprove it;
4. the smallest ablation experiment to test it;
5. pseudocode for the best idea.

## Latest Mechanism Result To Explain

Macro-chain witness is stronger than macro credit v2 because it changes the selected object:

- v2 gave local macro candidates a shallow future credit;
- macro-chain witness gives QUBO a short recovery-chain prefix with completion-witness evidence;
- receiver debt remains a penalty pressure;
- source scarcity helps judge whether a chain is structurally plausible;
- selected macro paths are reconstructed through compact path lifting and final validator replay.

Do not propose simply increasing/decreasing the same credit weights. The next improvement probably needs better chain quality, earlier primary hits, CV path-debt control, or stronger compact-lifting telemetry.

## Strong Hypotheses

### 1. The next bottleneck is chain quality, not chain availability

Macro-chain witness already brings future-bearing objects into QUBO. The next gain likely comes from ranking shorter chains, rejecting debt-heavy chains, or preferring chains whose suffix remains easy to polish.

### 2. BF and CV need separate diagnostics, not separate hard gates

BF now looks like an early-hit problem. CV looks like a path-length and route-debt problem. The mechanism can use structural features, but promotion tables must report BF and CV separately.

### 3. Compact reconstruction is part of the algorithm

Macro candidates are only useful if their selected compact path can be lifted back into legal crane moves. Any new mechanism should keep telemetry for generated chain, selected chain, repaired compact path, and final validator replay.

## Candidate Idea Menu

### Idea A: Chain-quality normalized QUBO credit

Normalize macro-chain credit by expected cleanup debt and chain length. Reward short witnessed chains that reduce `N` without increasing receiver debt.

Small test: BF27, CV/10-6, CV/10-10 plus open120 controls.

### Idea B: Early-hit reserve inside QUBO selection

Give a tiny QUBO-side preference to candidates with a high probability of reaching `N=0` soon, but only when they have replayable witness chains. This should be a selector preference, not a fallback solver.

Small test: BF27 and nearby BF controls.

### Idea C: CV path-debt near-tie veto

When two candidates have similar `N`, depth, and witness quality, veto the one with materially worse receiver debt or source-scarcity debt.

Small test: CV common-solved rows where macro-chain witness is longer than pure BS-FRG.

### Idea D: Macro-chain suffix polish witness

Before accepting a macro-chain candidate, run a very small proof that the suffix after the chain is not obviously trapped. Use only as scoring evidence.

Small test: CV/10-6 and CV/10-10.

### Idea E: Final-lineage dashboard

Summarize which selected macro chains survive to terminal paths, which are repaired during compact lifting, and which feature families appear on long CV paths.

Small test: BF27 plus the longest 20 CV common-solved rows.
